# Privacy Policy

_Placeholder — the final text will be provided by Hellogram's legal team. This notice is written for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023._

## What we collect

- Your mobile number (to verify you — never shown to anyone you chat with) and an optional recovery email.
- The numbers you create, your contact requests, messages and call history.

## Who can read your messages

Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are **not end-to-end encrypted**. When you report someone, the last 50 messages of that chat are shared with our safety team as evidence.

## How long we keep data

- Message text: per each chat's disappearing-message setting.
- Message and call metadata: 180 days.
- Report evidence: until the report is closed + 180 days.
- Billing records: 8 years (tax law).

## Your rights

Download your data or delete your account at any time from **Settings → Privacy & legal**. For anything else, contact our Grievance Officer.
